# Physics, Math and Statistical Reasoning review rubric

Review original chapter JSON, actual stage-specific diagrams and exact item IDs. Cite only sources actually inspected. Independent solving and keyed audit require separate inputs and passes. Report incomplete coverage with exact remaining items. Model reports are candidate evidence, not scientific sign-off.

- Systems and assumptions: identify boundary, frame, axes, sign conventions, starting conditions and idealization regime. Check constant acceleration, thin lenses, static fluids, laminar tubes and steady circuits where used.
- Dimensions: derive units, verify prefixes and squared/cubed conversions, distinguish dimensionless numbers and counts, check angles and significant-figure scope. A correct-looking number without appropriate meaning is insufficient.
- Vectors: verify components, direction, right-hand rules, object-specific free-body forces, lines of action, torque lever arms and scalar/vector distinctions.
- Conservation: account for energy transfer, work convention, dissipation, charge and nuclear equation balance. Distinguish net work from individual work and total entropy from a subsystem change.
- Models and figures: assess actual circuit connectivity, lens ray intersection/focal geometry, wave crest/trough/period/amplitude and field direction. A stage-specific replacement must be audited rather than assuming the exercise-wide image. Qualitative proportions are not numerical givens. Tracers show a named physical process, not measured speed.
- Calculations: independently justify the formula and assumptions, check substitution, signs, numerical precision, tolerance and limiting behavior. Software that repeats a key is not independent verification.
- Statistics: verify probability denominators, independence versus exclusivity, mean/median/weights, SD versus SE, distribution assumptions, p-value interpretation, Type I/II and power, interval/error-bar meaning and absolute/relative effects.
- Research: distinguish random sampling from allocation, technical from independent replication, intervention from observation, cohort from case-control, internal from external validity and association from causation. Ethical principles here are educational, not jurisdiction-specific advice.
- Assessments: confirm sufficient givens and a single intended defensible response, review every distractor, feedback and hint, prevent premature later-stage results, preserve meaningful signs and units, and keep assistance out of independent solves.
- Software evidence: textual review cannot certify actual animation, keyboard behavior, responsive geometry, browser storage or offline operation. Treat automated evidence and independent scientific conclusions as separate records.
